Example 4.8.22. Comparing the Growth Rates of \(\ln(x), x^2,\) and \(e^x\).

For each of the following pairs of functions, use L’Hôpital’s rule to evaluate \(\ds\lim_{x\to\infty}\frac{f(x)}{g(x)}\text{.}\)
🔗
  1. \(f(x)=x^2\) and \(g(x)=e^x\text{.}\)
    🔗
  2. \(f(x)=\ln(x)\) and \(g(x)=x^2\)
    🔗
Solution.
  1. Since \(\ds\lim_{x\to\infty}x^2=\infty\) and \(\ds\lim_{x\to\infty}e^x=\infty\text{,}\) we can use L’Hôpital’s rule to evaluate \(\ds\lim_{x\to\infty}\frac{x^2}{e^x}\text{.}\) We obtain
    \begin{equation*} \lim_{x\to\infty}\frac{x^2}{e^x}=\lim_{x\to\infty}\frac{2x}{e^x}\text{.} \end{equation*}
    Since \(\ds\lim_{x\to\infty}2x=\infty\) and \(\ds\lim_{x\to\infty}e^x=\infty\text{,}\) we can apply L’Hôpital’s rule again. Since
    \begin{equation*} \lim_{x\to\infty}\frac{2x}{e^x}=\lim_{x\to\infty}\frac{2}{e^x}=0\text{,} \end{equation*}
    we conclude that
    \begin{equation*} \lim_{x\to\infty}\frac{x^2}{e^x}=0\text{.} \end{equation*}
    Therefore, \(e^x\) grows more rapidly than \(x^2\) as \(x\to\infty\text{.}\) (See Figure 4.8.23 and Table 4.8.24).
    🔗
  2. Since \(\ds\lim_{x\to\infty}\ln{x}=\infty\) and \(\lim_{x\to\infty}x^2=\infty\text{,}\) we can use L’Hôpital’s rule to evaluate \(\ds\lim_{x\to\infty}\frac{\ln{x}}{x^2}\text{.}\) We obtain
    \begin{equation*} \lim_{x\to\infty}\frac{\ln{x}}{x^2}=\lim_{x\to\infty}\frac{1/x}{2x}=\lim_{x\to\infty}\frac{1}{2x^2}=0\text{.} \end{equation*}
    Thus, \(x^2\) grows more rapidly than \(\ln{x}\) as \(x\to\infty\) (See Figure 4.8.25 and Table 4.8.26).
    🔗
