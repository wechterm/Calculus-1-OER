Example 5.5.16 Using Substitution with an Exponential Function.

Use substitution to evaluate \(\displaystyle \int_{0}^{1} xe^{4x^2+3}\, dx.\)
🔗
Solution.
Let \(u=4x^3+3.\) Then, \(du=8x\, dx.\) To adjust the limits of integration, we note that when \(x=0\text{,}\) \(u=3,\) and when \(x=1\text{,}\) \(u=7.\) So our substitution gives
🔗
\begin{align*} \int_{0}^{1} xe^{4x^2+3}\, dx\amp =\frac{1}{8} \int_{3}^{7} e^u\, du\\ \amp =\frac{1}{8}e^u\biggr\vert_{u=3}^{u=7}\\ \amp =\frac{e^7-e^3}{8}\\ \amp \approx 134.568. \end{align*}
