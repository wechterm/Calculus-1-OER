Example 5.4.16 How Many Gallons of Gasoline Are Consumed?

If the motor on a motorboat is started at \(t = 0\) and the boat consumes gasoline at the rate of \(5 - t^3\) gal/hr, how much gasoline is used in the first \(2\) hours?
🔗
Solution.
Express the problem as a definite integral, integrate, and evaluate using the Fundamental Theorem of Calculus. The limits of integration are the endpoints of the interval \([0, 2]\text{.}\) We have
🔗
\begin{align*} \int_0^2 (5 - t^3)\, dt \amp= \left.\left(5t - \frac{t^4}{4}\right)\right|_0^2\\ \amp= \left[5(2) - \frac{(2)^4}{4}\right] - 0\\ \amp= 10 - \frac{16}{4}\\ \amp= 6. \end{align*}
Thus, the motorboat uses \(6\) gal of gas in \(2\) hours.
