Example 5.2.23 Finding the Total Area.

Find the total area between \(f(x)=x-2\) and the \(x\)-axis over the interval \([0,6].\)
🔗
Solution.
Calculate the \(x\)-intercept as \((2,0)\) (set \(y=0,\) solve for \(x\)). To find the total area, take the area below the \(x\)-axis over the subinterval \([0,2]\) and add it to the area above the \(x\)-axis on the subinterval \([2,6]\) (Figure 5.2.24).
🔗
We have
🔗
\begin{equation*} \displaystyle{\int_{0}^{6} |(x-2)|dx} =A_2+A_1. \end{equation*}
Then, using the formula for the area of a triangle, we obtain
🔗
\begin{equation*} A_2=\frac{1}{2}bh=\frac{1}{2}\cdot 2\cdot 2=2 \end{equation*}
\begin{equation*} A_1=\frac{1}{2}bh=\frac{1}{2}\cdot 4\cdot 4=8. \end{equation*}
The total area, then, is
🔗
\begin{equation*} A_1+A_2=8+2=10. \end{equation*}
