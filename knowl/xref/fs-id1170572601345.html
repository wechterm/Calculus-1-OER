Example 5.3.4 Finding a Derivative with the Fundamental Theorem of Calculus.

Use the Theorem 5.3.3 to find the derivative of
🔗
\begin{equation*} g(x)=\displaystyle{ \int_{1}^{x} \frac{1}{t^3+1}\, dt.} \end{equation*}
Solution.
According to the Fundamental Theorem of Calculus, the derivative is given by
🔗
\begin{equation*} g'(x) =\frac{1}{x^3+1}. \end{equation*}
